Vice Cover Studio — turn your photos into GTA 6-style multi-panel Vice City cover art, offline in the browser, and export it as a PNG.

Pick a layout (Classic hero + 3 panels, Quad 2×2, or Strip), load a photo into each panel, drag it to reposition and zoom to crop, or fill all panels with one photo. Type your title and subtitle, dial the Vice City sunset/neon grade, and export a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080.

Everything is original: the retro sun, palm silhouettes and lettering are drawn by the app itself — no Rockstar logos, artwork or fonts. The display type is Anton (SIL Open Font License, shipped in `app/fonts/`). Photos are read on your machine and never uploaded; the app makes no network requests at all.

Try it instantly: `?demo=1` loads the sample photos, `?view=cover` shows the cover alone.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
